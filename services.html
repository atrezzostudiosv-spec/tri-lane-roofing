HOW WE WORK

A CLEAR PROCESS, FROM START TO FINISH.

Tri-Lane's published roofing process outlines five distinct stages, including coordination with the customer and, when applicable, their insurance company.

01

Initial Contact

Contact the team to discuss your roofing needs. Tri-Lane gathers the initial information, answers questions, and arranges an on-site inspection.

02

Home Visit & Full Site Inspection

A roofing specialist visits the property to inspect the roof and document storm-related damage through detailed photographs and structural notes. When an insurance claim is involved, the team can assist with filing and coordinate directly with the insurance adjuster.

03

Project Review & Contract

After the applicable insurance claim has been processed, Tri-Lane reviews the scope of work, timeline, and costs with the customer. The team also discusses available material upgrades, possible out-of-pocket costs, and insurance estimates before providing a formal contract.

04

Scheduling & Installation

Following the signed contract and deposit, Tri-Lane coordinates material delivery and installation scheduling. The roofing crew performs the installation with project managers present to oversee safety and quality standards.

05

Final Inspection & Walkthrough

After the work is completed, the roof and worksite undergo a full quality review. A Tri-Lane representative then walks through the finished project with the customer to address all expectations.
